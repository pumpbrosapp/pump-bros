Gym Tonight Privacy Policy

App: Gym Tonight
Operated by: David Oliver Fairfax-Jones
Country: Luxembourg
Contact: pumpbrossupport@gmail.com
Effective date: October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data Gym Tonight collects, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept and what rights you have. It applies to the Gym Tonight app on iPhone and Android.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The controller of your personal data is David Oliver Fairfax-Jones, based in Luxembourg, who develops and operates Gym Tonight. For anything about your data, write to pumpbrossupport@gmail.com. Gym Tonight has not appointed a data protection officer, as it is not required to.

2. What we collect

Account

  • Email address and password (stored securely by our sign-in provider), or, if you use Sign in with Apple (iPhone), the name and email Apple shares with us. Apple may give us a private relay email instead of your real one.
  • When you accepted the Terms of Service, and which version.
  • Your date of birth is asked at sign-up only to check you are 16 or older. It is checked on your device and is never sent to us or stored.

Profile

  • Username, display name, profile picture, initials and profile colour.
  • XP, streaks, your workout split and your friend code.
  • Your notification settings and your device's time zone (for example "Europe/Luxembourg"), so days, streaks, weekly resets and reminders follow your local time.

Workouts

  • Each workout you log: its category (strength, cardio or other), the day of your split, the time and the XP earned.
  • Guided workouts (Premium): for each set you complete, the exercise, the day, the weight and the reps. This powers "last time", your strength charts and the progression guide. Only you can see it.
  • Fist bumps: who fist-bumped which workout, and when.
  • Notes you write on an exercise in a guided workout stay only on your device and are not sent to us.

Health data (optional)

Only if you choose to connect Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android), and only after you agree on the consent screen that appears first: - We read your step count and the workouts recorded on your device (type, start time and length). We never write to, change or delete your health data, and we don't ask for anything else (no heart rate, sleep, weight or location). - We store one row per day with your step total and the XP it earned, and a yes/no flag per day saying whether Health recorded a qualifying gym or cardio session. A cardio session of 15 minutes or more is logged as a cardio workout. Individual health samples and workout details stay on your device. - Friends see only a "Verified" label on the workouts that Health confirmed. Your step count is visible only to you.

Friends, groups and messages

  • Friend requests and friendships, groups you create or join (name, photo, invite code, privacy setting, members), group invites and weekly group results.
  • Direct and group messages: the text, any photo you send, who sent it to whom, when, and when it was delivered and read. Records of messages you've hidden from your own view.
  • Leaderboard events (for example a friend passing you) used for notifications.

Safety

  • Users you block (visible only to you).
  • Reports you submit: the reason, the account and, if applicable, the message reported.
  • If an account is suspended: when, and the reason.

Premium subscription

  • Which plan you have, purchase, renewal and expiry dates, trial status, whether auto-renew is on, cancellations, refunds and billing problems, and the store's transaction identifiers. We receive this from Apple or Google via RevenueCat. We never see your card or bank details.
  • Exercise demo videos (Premium): when you open an exercise that has a 3D demo video, the app downloads it from our storage provider (Supabase) through a short-lived link tied to your signed-in account, so only Premium accounts can load it. Like any request to our servers, the provider keeps standard technical logs of these downloads. We don't build a history of which videos you watch.

Notifications

  • If you allow notifications: your device's push token, its platform and when it was last updated.

Stored only on your device

  • Notes on exercises (see above), the home-screen widget's copy of your training days and split (to show your streak), your app settings (for example dark mode), and the demo videos you've opened (cached so they play offline; the cache is cleared when Premium ends). None of this is sent to us; deleting the app removes it.

Crash reports

  • When the app crashes or hits an error: technical details (device model, operating system, app version, what the app was doing) and your account ID. We do not send your name or email with crash reports.

What we don't collect

No precise location, contacts, advertising identifiers, browsing history, microphone or camera recordings, and no payment details. We don't use any remote AI service. The photo library is accessed only when you pick a photo to upload.

3. Why we use it, and on what legal basis

Purpose Legal basis (GDPR)
Creating your account, signing you in, your profile, workouts, XP, streaks, splits, leaderboards, friends, groups and messages Performance of our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b))
Premium: checking and managing your subscription, guided workouts, strength charts Performance of our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b))
Reading Apple Health / Health Connect and showing the Verified label Your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) and 6(1)(a)); you can withdraw it at any time
Push notifications Your consent through the system permission (Art. 6(1)(a))
The 16+ age check at sign-up Legitimate interest in keeping under-16s off the service (Art. 6(1)(f))
Blocking, reporting, the word filter, reviewing reports and suspending accounts Legitimate interest in keeping users safe, and our legal obligations as a hosting service (Art. 6(1)(f) and (c))
Crash reports and keeping the app secure Legitimate interest in a working, secure app (Art. 6(1)(f))
Answering support and privacy requests, keeping records the law requires Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c))

We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

Automated filtering: messages, group names and profile names are checked against a list of prohibited words before they are sent or saved. If there's a match, the text is not sent. This doesn't make any other decision about you; suspensions are always decided by a person.

4. Who can see your data

  • Other users: your profile, XP, streak, split and leaderboard position are visible to signed-in users. Friends see your workouts from the last 7 days (with the Verified label where it applies). Messages are visible to the people in the conversation or group. Your profile picture and group photos can be opened by anyone who has the link; chat photos can only be opened by the people in that chat, through links that expire after an hour.
  • Our service providers, who process data only on our instructions:
  • Supabase (database, sign-in, file storage, server functions);
  • RevenueCat (subscription status; receives your account ID and purchase information);
  • Sentry (crash reports, processed in the EU, Germany);
  • Expo (delivering push notifications; receives the notification text and your push token);
  • Apple and Google (Sign in with Apple, in-app payments, Apple Health / Health Connect and push delivery, under their own privacy policies).
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

5. International transfers

Some providers (RevenueCat and Expo) are based in the United States. Transfers to them rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, or on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account, profile, workouts, sets, health rows, friends, groups, messages, blocks, reports, push tokens and subscription records: until you delete your account (or, for a message, until the sender deletes it). Deleting your account removes them, together with your profile picture and chat photos, and asks RevenueCat to delete your customer record.
  • Crash reports: up to 90 days at Sentry.
  • Backups: deleted data can remain in our providers' backups for a short time (normally no more than 30 days) until they are overwritten.
  • Apple and Google keep their own purchase records under their own policies.

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a Premium subscription; cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings first.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its use, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw consent at any time (withdrawing doesn't affect what was done before).

  • Delete your account: Profile › Privacy & data › Delete account. Can't open the app? See https://pumpbrosapp.github.io/pump-bros/delete-account.html or email us.
  • Stop Health access: iPhone: Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Gym Tonight. Android: Health Connect › App permissions › Gym Tonight.
  • Turn off notifications: in Gym Tonight's notification settings or your phone's settings.
  • Everything else (for example a copy of your data): email pumpbrossupport@gmail.com. We answer within one month.

You can also complain to the Luxembourg data protection authority, the Commission nationale pour la protection des données (CNPD), 15, Boulevard du Jazz, L-4370 Belvaux, www.cnpd.lu, or to the authority where you live.

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Access to the database is restricted by row-level security so each user can only read what the app allows them to. Sensitive operations (XP, account deletion, moderation) run on the server. No system is perfectly secure; tell us at once if you think your account has been compromised.

9. Age requirement

Gym Tonight is only for people 16 and older. The sign-up age check is based on the date of birth you enter. If you believe someone under 16 has an account, email pumpbrossupport@gmail.com and we will remove it.

10. Changes

We will update this Policy when the app or the law changes, and change the date at the top. If a change significantly affects how we use your data, we will tell you in the app.
