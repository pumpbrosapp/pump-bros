Delete your Gym Tonight account

You can delete your account and its data at any time, from inside the app or by email.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Gym Tonight and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Privacy & data, then Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

By email

Can't open the app? Email pumpbrossupport@gmail.com from the address you signed up with (or tell us your username) and ask us to delete your account. We do it within 30 days, normally much sooner, and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account, sign-in details and profile (name, username, picture).
  • Your workouts, sets, XP, streaks, split and step history.
  • Friendships, group memberships, messages you sent and chat photos.
  • Blocks and reports you made, push tokens, and your subscription record at RevenueCat.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Copies in our providers' backups, for up to 30 days until they are overwritten.
  • Crash reports at Sentry, for up to 90 days (they contain only your account ID, no name or email).
  • Apple and Google keep their own purchase records under their own policies.

Premium

Deleting your account does not cancel a Premium subscription. Cancel it first in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.
